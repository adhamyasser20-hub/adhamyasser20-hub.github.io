About me

Practical by habit, curious by choice.

أنا أدهم ياسر، من إسكندرية.

I'm Adham, from Alexandria. My day job is in purchasing and warehouse management, so I spend my time with stock sheets, suppliers and numbers that have to add up.

On the side I build things: Excel workbooks with Arabic interfaces, books for travelers and shop owners, and story videos. I use AI tools along the way and check the result myself before anything goes out.

The idea is simple. Make small useful things, put them out, learn from what happens, and make the next one better.
